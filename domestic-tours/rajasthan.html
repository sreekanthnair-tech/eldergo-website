Why Rajasthan?

A journey through royal heritage, vibrant culture, timeless cities and the golden desert — from the lakes of Udaipur and the Blue City of Jodhpur to the Sam Dunes and the Pink City of Jaipur, at a comfortable pace with everything taken care of.

  • Grand forts & palaces — Mehrangarh, Amber Fort and the City Palaces
  • Two nights at a desert camp near the Sam Dunes, with a sunset camel ride
  • Living culture — folk dance, the Bishnoi village and Mandawa’s painted havelis

Questions About This Tour

Is this Rajasthan tour physically demanding?
The pace is relaxed, with rest at the hotel after lunch on most sightseeing days. Some travel days involve long drives (about 5.5 to 7 hours on Days 3, 5, 7 and 8) in an AC coach, with planned breaks for refreshments and stretching. Forts and palaces involve some walking on uneven surfaces and steps; there is lift access at Mehrangarh Fort and a jeep ride up to Amber Fort. You can skip any activity if you need extra rest.
What is the weather like in December and January?
Days can be pleasant to warm, while evenings and early mornings can be very cold, especially at the desert camp in December. Please carry warm layers; our team will share a packing list.
Are flights included in the price?
No. Prices are for the land package only. Flights are extra and quoted based on your date of departure and departure city. The tour starts in Udaipur and ends in Jaipur, and our team will help you with flights.
Are the camel ride, jeep safari and ATV rides included?
The sunset camel ride on the Sam Dunes is included in the Comfort package. The jeep safari and ATV rides are optional and payable directly. Desert activities depend on weather and local conditions.
What is the food like?
All meals are included as per the day-wise plan. Many hotels and restaurants in Rajasthan mainly serve vegetarian food, and Indian food options are available for breakfast and dinner. Please share any dietary needs when you enquire.
What if I need to cancel?
Full refund if cancelled 45+ days before the trip start date, 50% between 45 and 31 days, and no refund within 30 days. For last-minute cancellations we also try to find a replacement traveller so you can recover your amount. Cancellation applies to the land package only — flight cancellations follow the airline’s policy. Full policy available on request.
Is the tour manager with us the entire time?
Yes — a first-aid trained ElderGo tour manager is with you from Day 1 to Day 10, supported by local guides in Udaipur, Jodhpur and Jaipur.
