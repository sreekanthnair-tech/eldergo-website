Why Andaman?

Turquoise water, powder-white sand and a slow island rhythm — the Andamans are where the sea becomes part of your holiday, not just a place you visit for an hour. From the moving history of the Cellular Jail to the celebrated shores of Radhanagar, this is a journey made for unhurried days and easy company.

  • Radhanagar, Elephant & Kalapathar — the islands' most beautiful beaches
  • The Cellular Jail Light & Sound Show — India's freedom story
  • Island boat excursions, coral reefs and a farewell dinner cruise

Your 6-Day Journey

DAY 1 / 6 Arrive Port Blair

Welcome to the Islands

Arrival L·D 4★ Premium Port Blair

Your islands begin the moment you land at Port Blair. After a warm welcome and lunch, settle into your beach-side hotel, then head out to Corbyn Cove for a gentle sunset by the sea.

As evening falls, the historic Cellular Jail comes alive with the moving Light & Sound Show — India's freedom story told under the stars — before a relaxed dinner and rest.

Arrive: Port Blair
Meals: Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel, Port Blair
DAY 2 / 6 Port Blair

Jolly Buoy / Red Skin — A Day in the Blue

Boat day B·L·D 4★ Premium Port Blair

An early start today, with a packed breakfast, for the day's big adventure. Drive to Wandoor and board a boat to Jolly Buoy / Red Skin Island — a protected marine world of clear water, coral reefs and vibrant sea life (the island varies with the season).

Return to Port Blair for lunch, then visit the Anthropological Museum to understand the islands' indigenous communities and their rich cultural history.

Wandoor · island boat ride
Meals: B, L & D
4★ Premium Hotel, Port Blair
DAY 3 / 6 Port Blair Havelock

Sail to Havelock — Beaches & Sunset

Ferry 2 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Havelock

After breakfast, a visit to the Cellular Jail deepens the story you glimpsed the first evening. Then down to the harbour to sail across to Havelock Island, watching Port Blair slip away and the tropical blue open up.

Lunch and check-in at your beach resort, and in the evening, the celebrated Radhanagar Beach — soft white sand and a sunset over turquoise water that stays with you.

Ferry: Port Blair → Havelock (2 hrs)
Meals: B, L & D
4★ Premium Beach Resort, Havelock
DAY 4 / 6 Havelock

Into the Blue — Elephant & Kalapathar

Boat + beaches B·L·D 4★ Premium Havelock

A full, unhurried day on Havelock. Cruise across the bay to Elephant Beach, ringed by tropical greenery — those who wish can add water activities (payable directly), while others simply enjoy the sand, a coconut and the sea.

Later, the quieter Kalapathar Beach, with golden sand and a beautiful island backdrop, is the perfect place to slow down.

Boat: Elephant Beach
Meals: B, L & D
4★ Premium Beach Resort, Havelock
DAY 5 / 6 Havelock Port Blair

Shopping & an Evening at Sea

Ferry 2 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Port Blair

One last Havelock breakfast before the ferry carries you back to Port Blair, blue waters all the way. After lunch, explore Aberdeen Bazaar for souvenirs, handicrafts and gifts to carry home.

Then the evening you'll remember — a farewell Dinner Cruise on the Andaman Sea, with dinner, music and the coastline glowing at dusk.

Ferry: Havelock → Port Blair (2 hrs)
Meals: B, L & D
4★ Premium Hotel, Port Blair

Questions About This Tour

Is the Andaman tour physically demanding?
It's a relaxed, senior-friendly pace with comfortable AC coach transfers in Port Blair and Ertiga vehicles in Havelock. There is some gentle walking at sightseeing spots and jetties, plus a few boat and ferry rides. Optional water sports are entirely your choice.
Are flights included in the price?
No. Pricing is land-only. Flights are extra and quoted based on your date of departure and departure city — our team will help you book them.
What are the meals like?
All meals are included as per the day-wise plan, planned with the senior palate in mind — mostly at the hotel, with vetted restaurants when dining out. Andaman cuisine leans Bengali and South Indian; come with an open mind and enjoy the local flavours.
Can I do the water sports, scuba and snorkelling?
Water sports at Elephant Beach are optional and payable directly. Several underwater activities have age restrictions and may require a doctor's fitness certificate; operators may decline participation for guests above 60 at their discretion.
What is the transport like on the islands?
A comfortable AC coach in Port Blair and Ertiga vehicles (maximum 4 guests, seating rotated) in Havelock. Andaman's roads are narrow, so coaches are more compact than on the mainland — but the pace stays relaxed.
Is the tour manager with us the entire time?
Yes — a first-aid trained ElderGo tour manager is with you from Day 1 to Day 6, taking care of transfers, ferries, hotels and sightseeing.
