Why Sri Lanka?

An island where ancient kingdoms, misty tea mountains and golden beaches sit within easy reach — Sri Lanka packs extraordinary variety into a gentle, senior-friendly journey, all at a relaxed pace with everything taken care of.

  • Ancient cities, cave temples & UNESCO World Heritage sites
  • Misty tea country, waterfalls & tropical beaches
  • Rich culture — Buddhist temples & the Ramayana trail

Your 8-Day Journey

DAY 1 / 8 Colombo Negombo

Welcome to Sri Lanka

30 min L·D 5★ Negombo

Arrive at Colombo (Bandaranaike) International Airport, where your ElderGo Tour Manager welcomes you. After lunch, transfer the short distance to Negombo on the west coast.

Take a relaxed stroll along Negombo Beach for your first glimpse of Sri Lanka’s tropical coast, then visit the colourful Angurukaramulla Temple, known for its large reclining Buddha. Dinner and rest.

Drive: 30 minutes
Meals: Lunch & Dinner
5★ Hotel
DAY 2 / 8 Negombo Sigiriya

Sigiriya — Ancient Wonder

4–5 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Sigiriya

After breakfast, begin the scenic drive towards Sigiriya, stopping for lunch en route.

Explore the beautifully planned Sigiriya Water Gardens and discover the fascinating story of the rock fortress at the Sigiriya Museum.

Optional: the Lion Rock summit climb is available for interested guests and is payable directly.

Drive: 4–5 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 3 / 8 Sigiriya Dambulla

Village Life & Ancient Caves

2 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Sigiriya

Step into rural Sri Lanka on a village tour, experiencing local traditions and everyday village life, followed by an authentic traditional village lunch prepared with local flavours.

In the afternoon, explore the remarkable Dambulla Cave Temple — a complex of ancient caves filled with Buddha statues, intricate paintings and centuries of heritage. Return to the hotel for dinner.

Drive: 2 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 4 / 8 Sigiriya Kandy

Kandy — Culture, Tradition & Heritage

4 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Kandy

Travel through Sri Lanka’s lush central highlands to the hill capital of Kandy, with lunch and hotel check-in on arrival.

Experience the vibrant Kandyan Cultural Show of traditional music and dance, then visit the revered Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, one of the island’s most important Buddhist sites. Dinner and rest.

Drive: 4 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 5 / 8 Kandy Nuwara Eliya

Into the Tea Country

3–4 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Nuwara Eliya

Drive into the misty hills towards Nuwara Eliya. Visit the Bhakth Hanuman Temple, an important stop on the Ramayana trail.

Pause at the spectacular Ramboda Falls, then visit a tea factory to see how Ceylon tea is grown and prepared. Hotel check-in and relax.

Note: the Hanuman temple involves about 800m of flat walking and roughly 50 steps to the main shrine.

Drive: 3–4 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 6 / 8 Nuwara Eliya

Following the Ramayana Route

2 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Nuwara Eliya

Visit the sacred Sita Amman Temple, then Ashok Vatika and historic Divurumpola — key sites along the Ramayana trail.

Unwind by scenic Gregory Lake and enjoy the cool, peaceful atmosphere of Nuwara Eliya before returning to the hotel for dinner.

Note: Sita Amman Temple involves a flight of roughly 60 steps to the elevated sanctum.

Drive: 2 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 7 / 8 Nuwara Eliya Colombo

Colombo — Beach, Markets & City Life

5 hrs B·L·D 4★ Premium Colombo

Bid farewell to the cool hills and drive back to Sri Lanka’s vibrant capital, Colombo, with lunch en route and hotel check-in.

Enjoy some relaxed beach leisure and browse the bustling Pettah Market before returning to the hotel for dinner.

Drive: 5 hours
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
4★ Premium Hotel
DAY 8 / 8 Colombo Airport

Until We Meet Again

1 hr B·L Return Flight

After breakfast and check-out, visit the iconic Independence Square and the distinctive Gangaramaya Temple, known for its fascinating collection of art and artefacts.

Enjoy a farewell lunch before your transfer to the airport for your return flight — carrying home memories of misty mountains, ancient stories and wonderful moments together.

Drive: 1 hour
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Return Flight

Questions About This Tour

Is this Sri Lanka tour physically demanding?
Most of the journey is comfortable with AC coach transfers and a relaxed, senior-friendly pace. Some sites involve stairs — around 50 steps at Bhakth Hanuman Temple and about 60 steps at Sita Amman Temple. The Sigiriya Lion Rock climb is optional and payable directly; you can skip it entirely.
What’s the weather like?
Sri Lanka is warm and tropical along the coast and in the ancient cities, while the hill country around Nuwara Eliya is cool and misty. Temperatures generally range from the low 20s to low 30s°C. Your travel advisor can recommend the best month for your preferences.
Is the Sigiriya Lion Rock climb included?
The Lion Rock climb is optional and payable directly for interested guests. The included programme covers the Sigiriya Water Gardens and Museum, so you can enjoy the site without climbing.
What if I need to cancel?
Full refund if cancelled 30+ days before departure. We also try to find a replacement traveller for last-minute cancellations to help you get your money back. Full cancellation policy available on request.
Is the tour manager with us the entire time?
Yes — a first-aid trained ElderGo tour manager accompanies you from Day 1 to Day 8, supported by local guides for cultural context and expertise.
I have dietary requirements — can you accommodate?
We cater to vegetarian and common dietary preferences on all our tours. Please let us know your requirements when you enquire and we’ll confirm what we can accommodate for your specific tour.
